Soft Skills

BCA (Hons) – Semester II | Subject Code: U32F7SS

Academic Year: 2024-25

Teaching & Examination Scheme
  • Lecture Hours: 2 per week
  • Credits: 2
  • Continuous Evaluation (CE): 25 Marks
  • Semester End Exam (SEE): 25 Marks
  • Total: 50 Marks
Objective

The objective of this course is to teach communicative English, to develop the soft skills suitable to professional course to the students by applying practical tools

Pre-requisites

Basic knowledge of communication skills and soft skills.

Course Outcomes
  • CO1: To increase the employability of the students.
  • CO2: Learn basics of business etiquette to deal with real life problems
  • CO3: Understand & learn depth about leadership skills.
  • CO4: To develop professionalism in diverse work environments.
  • CO5: Acquire valuable soft skills through studying leadership examples.
