Effective Writing Skills

BCA (Hons) – Semester II | Subject Code: U32D5EWS

Academic Year: 2024-25

Teaching & Examination Scheme
  • Lecture Hours: 2 per week
  • Credits: 2
  • Continuous Evaluation (CE): 25 Marks
  • Semester End Exam (SEE): 25 Marks
  • Total: 50 Marks
Objective

Understand the role of communication in personal & professional success. Develop awareness of appropriate communication strategies. Prepare and present messages with specific intent. Analyze communication acts and ethically integrate sources.

Pre-requisites

Familiarity with the basics of English language, strong determination and willpower for skill-set enhancement.

Course Outcomes (CO)
  • CO1: Understand and apply complex vocabulary and grammar structures
  • CO2: Understand and analyze various nuances of writing
  • CO3: Understand and evaluate different forms of formal writing and produce effective writing
  • CO4: Understand, analyze and create social media writing
  • CO5: Apply knowledge of communication skills and produce effective responses
