Writing is a Skill

Writing helps express ideas, thoughts, and communication clearly.

  • It is a productive skill.
  • Requires continuous practice.
  • Reading and listening improve writing.
  • Practice daily to become better.

Consequences of Poor Writing

Meet Mr. Pasha

Dear Manager Saheb,

I will not come because my eyes have come. If I come with my eyes coming then your eyes will come.

Still if you come with your coming eyes all eyes will come.

Yours sincerely,
Pasha Bhai

Moral: Don't become like Mr. Pasha. Start improving your writing skills.
