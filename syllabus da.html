Objective

The goal of the Advanced Microsoft Excel course is to help students become more proficient analysts and complicated data managers by exploring some of Excel's more advanced features. Students will investigate pivot tables and learn effective methods for condensing, analyzing, and presenting data. Additionally, they will learn how to use data tools for data translation and validation, become proficient at auditing worksheets to verify accuracy, and comprehend how to work cooperatively with others while safeguarding sensitive information.

Pre-requisites

Students should know basic understanding of Excel.

Course Outcomes (CO)
  • CO1: Understand If data validation and analysis.
  • CO2: Understand advanced functions and formulas.
  • CO3: Demonstrate and understand how to use pivot tables to analyze data.
  • CO4: Understand advance analysis tool and data visualization.
  • CO5: Understand and build an Excel Dashboard.
