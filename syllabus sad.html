Software Analysis and Design

BCA (Hons) – Semester II

Subject Code: U32B3SAD | Credits: 4

Academic Year: 2024-25

Teaching & Examination Scheme
  • Lecture Hours: 4 per week
  • Credits: 4
  • Continuous Evaluation (CE): 50 Marks
  • Semester End Exam (SEE): 50 Marks
  • Total: 100 Marks
Objective

Students will be able to comprehend various software process models, the software engineering development process, UML diagrams, project planning, project scheduling, and software risk analysis at the end of the course

Pre-requisites

The fundamentals of how software is developed should be well understood by the students.

Course Outcomes
  • CO1: Gain a comprehensive understanding of software engineering principles, focusing on systematic analysis, design, Software Development Life Cycle phases, and effective Requirements Engineering.
  • CO2: Design and analyze systems using OOAD principles and Solve real-world problems through case studies.
  • CO3: Understand the principles of software architecture, Analyze and design software systems with a focus on architectural patterns.
  • CO4: Analyze case studies to identify testing and QA best practices.
  • CO5: Understanding project management principles, creating project plans and estimates for software projects, and identifying and managing risks in such projects.
