Credits: 4 | Lecture: 2 hrs | Practical: 4 hrs | Total Marks: 100

Teaching & Examination Scheme
  • Lecture: 2 Hours per week
  • Practical: 4 Hours per week
  • Total Hours: 6
  • Theory: CE – 50 | SEE – 50 | Total – 100 Marks
Objective

After the completion of the course, students are able to learn Web and HTML concepts and develop websites using different tools.

Pre-requisites

An intermediate knowledge on HTML, JavaScript and CSS.

Course Outcomes (CO)
  • CO1: Understanding the use of JQuery selectors and Animations and Effects in web pages.
  • CO2: Understand the concept of Responsive web page, Explore different bootstrap layouts and grid systems.
  • CO3: Create dynamic updating content using css3 and Media Queries.
  • CO4: Implement modal bootstrap in a web page.
  • CO5: Understand the fundamentals of JavaScript.
